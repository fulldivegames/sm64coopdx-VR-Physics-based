Physical Jumping

Enabled by default for new settings; toggle in VR Settings > Immersion > Movement & Body.
The first 0.9.2 launch enables physical actions once (not crouching). You can
disable them again; subsequent launches preserve that choice.
While Mario is on land, hold grip on either controller and punch straight
upward toward/above head height. Trigger is not required. Keep that hand raised
to hold jump; pull it back down or release grip for a shorter jump.
The normal jump binding continues to work independently.
Disable Punch Sound, directly under Physical Jumping, mutes only the physical
punch voice. Jump and jump-kick sounds are unchanged. It is off by default.
Use Triggers Instead of Grips switches this gesture to triggers, including
wall kicks. It is off by default; change your crouch binding before enabling.
Grabbing and physical punching continue to use grips.
Punching both hands up together triggers one jump, not two. Either raised hand
can sustain that jump; lower both hands or release both grips to shorten it.

Lower and punch again after landing, using either hand or alternating hands.
This supplies normal jump-button presses, preserving the game's speed/timing
requirements for double/triple jumps. Triple jumps retain their native arc.
Keeping a hand raised through landing does not cause automatic hopping.
A completed gesture up to 120 ms before landing is buffered until contact.
Physical jump chains allow about 67 ms extra after the usual late window.
This does not permit free midair jumps or remove the triple-jump speed rule.
The upward gesture also performs wall kicks within the normal wall-kick window.
Keep the triggering fist raised to sustain the full wall kick. Lower it or
release the control for a shorter kick; landing clears the hold.
With Propeller Mushroom active, another physical jump gesture in the air
activates its burst. Lower/rearm the hand first, or use the other hand.

The gesture is disabled in menus, underwater, while physically climbing,
while holding an object, and during cutscenes/death. Tracking loss and
recentering clear pending gestures. Recognized upward jump strokes do not
also queue an ordinary physical punch from the same hand.

Initial detection tuning: 12 cm upward travel within 0.4 seconds, upward
speed at least 0.85 m/s, hand reaching within 15 cm below headset height.
The punch must remain within 15 degrees of straight up until it triggers;
forward/diagonal motion resets the pending gesture.
Lowering 10 cm from the raised peak releases jump; lowering 14 cm rearms.
These are tracking-space distances, independent of Big Hands' visual reach.
