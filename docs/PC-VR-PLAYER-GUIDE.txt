SM64 CO-OP DX VR v0.9.24 - ORIGINAL COLOR AND DISPLAY CONTROLS HOTFIX

Brightness and contrast are now together under VR > Display > Effects. The
update applies the neutral 100% / 100% color baseline once, then keeps the
settings you choose afterward. PC red tones receive a small correction to
better match the standalone build.

The first 0.9.2 launch enables physical jumping, swimming, punching, grabbing,
climbing, swing release and ground/air motion dives once. Crouching and bindings
are unchanged. Disable any unwanted actions in settings; later launches retain
your changes. Raised fists sustain full wall kicks as well as ordinary jumps.

Extract the complete ZIP into its own folder and launch SM64-Co-Op-DX-VR.exe.
Keep all included DLLs and resource folders beside the executable.
This VR build always uses OpenGL, including flat-screen startup. Old DirectX 11
settings and renderer command-line overrides are ignored. This does not change
Windows, SteamVR or Virtual Desktop settings; DirectX-only hardware is unsupported.
You must supply your own legally obtained Super Mario 64 US ROM.
If no valid ROM is found, an in-game folder browser opens and attempts to
start OpenXR so setup can appear in your connected headset before gameplay.
Use either thumbstick to move, A or trigger to open, B to go back, and the
menu/view button to return to locations. Select your US Super Mario 64 .z64.
The browser lists Downloads, Home, the game folder, and available drives.
On Proton, Z: provides access to the host filesystem if your ROM is elsewhere.
For PC streaming, select the ROM on the PC; for local Proton, select it on
the headset filesystem. Steam Frame hardware has not been tested.
Gamepad A/B and D-pad also work. Keyboard fallback: arrows, Enter, Backspace,
Escape (locations). Drag-and-drop remains supported. No Windows dialog is used.
The game can reuse your previously supplied ROM from its user-data folder
(normally AppData/Roaming/sm64coopdx), so updates do not require another copy.

Connect your headset using your OpenXR runtime. SteamVR supports Steam Link
and ALVR. Enable VR in the game's settings. Controller bindings are available
in VR settings; the default pause input is the left thumbstick click.

This update's main change is batched renderer uploads for large maps. It also
includes Big Hands in the special spawn pool, with extended hand interactions
and climbing. Performance gains depend on your map and hardware.

Optional normal maps can be enabled in VR display effects. They are disabled
by default. Special power-ups remain unavailable in Regular Public Lobbies.

Use the in-game update option for future PC VR releases. A separate upstream
coopdx_updater.exe is intentionally not included. Do not mix this package
with a regular flat-screen Co-op DX installation.

Your existing save data and settings are not included in this package.

CURRENT FEATURES
Propeller Mushroom lasts 60 seconds: jump again in the air (button or physical
jump gesture) for one burst per airtime. Hold crouch for fast descent and a
3x-speed opaque tornado; release to return to slow glide. Ordinary twirling
is unchanged. See PROPELLER-MUSHROOM.txt and the in-game Special Moves tutorial.
The in-game VR Tutorial covers Big Hands, speedrunning, visual options and caps.
Big Hands uses the enlarged hands for grabbing and physical punches. Hold Grip
at a surface and pull to climb; alternate hands between supported surfaces,
trees and hangables. Release Grip to detach. Special powers are disabled in
Regular Public Lobbies. Big Hands lasts 30 seconds by default; enable Big Hands
60-Second Timer in Special Moves for a 60-second duration. The duration is
captured when the power-up is collected, so changing the option never alters
an active pickup.

VR > Speedrunning contains timer setup, split names, size, position and color.
Choose Split Count and Confirm. Show Timer and Splits enables the viewer;
Reveal Splits as You Go adds the next row after each split. Assign Timer Start /
Split in Controller Bindings. See VR-SPEEDRUN.txt for PC/Quest setup imports.

Special > Filters includes Cell Shaded and the grayscale Super Mario Land
filter. Character Select uses a floating theater panel for its menu and preview.
Immersion includes crossed, stationary tree billboards. With Grab Cap at Any
Time enabled, hold Grip and Trigger near your head to remove the hat. Either
button can be pressed first. The life icon shows a red X when the hat is absent.

PC keyboard dictation runs locally using the included speech folder. Press Mic,
speak, then press Mic again to finish and transcribe; keep the keyboard open
while it processes. Closing the keyboard cancels. Set your headset microphone
as the default recording device. See speech/PC-DICTATION.txt for details.

CURRENT LOCAL GESTURES AND SETTINGS

VR > Immersion > Movement & Body:
- Physical Swimming defaults on for new settings. Reach outward, then pull water back
  toward your upper torso. Normal pulls follow headset aim. Pull down from
  overhead to swim upward. Recovery produces no thrust. Either hand,
  alternating hands and both hands work. Normal button swimming remains.
- "Use Triggers Instead of Grips" switches physical jumping and wall kicks
  to either trigger. Off by default. Change crouch binding before enabling;
  grabbing and punching still use grips. Hard climb-release swings now give
  the full jump-off ascent; soft swings and ordinary drops are unchanged.
- Physical Jumping defaults on for new settings. Hold Grip and punch within 15 degrees
  of straight up while grounded. Trigger is not needed. Either/both hands can
  jump; keep one raised to hold Jump, or lower/release to shorten it. Lower and
  punch again after landing for normal double/triple jumps.
- Physical Crouching / Ground Pounds is on by default. Physical Crouch Depth
  defaults to 10%, adjustable from 10 to 50. Lower values need less crouching.
  Head-tilt compensation reduces accidental crouching when looking down.
  The setting uses calibrated height; recenter while standing neutrally.
- Existing saved settings are retained. New defaults do not overwrite them.

Special power-up spawn weights range from 1 to 100, default 50. These are
relative weights, not literal percentages. Disable a power-up with its checkbox.
Big Hands lasts 30 seconds; its optional 60-second timer applies at pickup.
Big Hands has half the relative box-spawn weight of ordinary power-ups at
equal slider settings, even with its optional 60-second timer enabled.
Physical Jumping also wall-kicks; its 120 ms landing buffer helps jump chains.
Crossed Tree Billboards defaults on; Look Toward Spawned Star defaults off.
Normal maps remain optional and off by default.

Controller Bindings captures the next chosen input. Right-primary still
confirms menus even if Jump is remapped. Pause defaults to left stick click.
Mic starts dictation; press it again to finish, rather than cancel. Closing the
keyboard cancels. Quest uses Android dictation; PC uses its bundled speech files.

Speedrunning has its own VR menu for setup, names, split count, Confirm, HUD
position/size/color and progressive split reveal. See VR-SPEEDRUN.txt.
Special > Filters includes Cell Shaded and Super Mario Land. Character Select
uses a floating theater panel. See PHYSICAL-SWIMMING.txt and PHYSICAL-JUMPING.txt
for gesture details. These are local-build instructions, not a release notice.
Propeller Mushroom lasts 60 seconds: jump again in the air (button or physical
jump gesture) for one burst per airtime. Hold crouch for fast descent and a
3x-speed opaque tornado; release to glide. Ordinary twirling has no crouch drill.
Its helmet follows the head and is hidden in first person. Customize/export
the Propeller palette: Cap = helmet, Emblem = rotor. See PROPELLER-MUSHROOM.txt.
The Hammer Suit helmet and back shell follow Mario's body. A top stomp on a normally stompable
enemy relaunches Propeller Mario at full burst strength; side hits and Bullies do not.

Power Star is a small bouncing pickup with its own Super Mario Bros. 3 Star Theme.
It gives 30 seconds of enemy-contact invincibility, 1.5x running speed and 1.5x
jump height. Its rainbow tint and music fade over the last four seconds, while
abilities continue until expiry. Pits and scripted deaths still apply.
Enable Power Star 60-Second Timer before pickup for the longer duration.
Power-Up Spawn/Enable groups Big Hands and Power Star under Specials; both
have half the normal spawn weight. Rasengan / Rasen-Shuriken are under Gestures
and have no spawn weight. Native caps and dedicated power-up songs keep their
own music regardless of the alternate-music setting.
