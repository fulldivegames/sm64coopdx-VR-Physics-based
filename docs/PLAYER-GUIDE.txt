SM64 CO-OP DX VR STANDALONE v0.6.0 - QUEST PLAYER GUIDE
================================================

WHAT YOU NEED

- A Meta Quest 3 with Developer Mode enabled
- Your own legally obtained, unmodified Super Mario 64 US ROM
- A PC or Android phone for installing the APK

Nintendo ROMs and game assets are not included in this download.
The ROM and extracted Nintendo assets are never included in the APK.


FIRST-TIME SETUP

1. Install the APK with SideQuest or ADB.
2. Launch SM64 Co-Op DX VR Standalone from Unknown Sources.
3. Use the system file picker to select your legally obtained US .z64 ROM.
4. Wait for validation and asset preparation to finish.
5. Open Camera Settings if you want to adjust first-person height or comfort.

The game remembers the validated ROM in its user-data folder.


CAMERA MODES

- Third Person Mode keeps the character visible. It works with the original
  camera and SM64 Co-Op DX's free camera. Camera distance is adjustable.
- First Person Mode is the default. The headset controls the view and Mario's
  forward direction. The camera stick provides smooth horizontal turning.
- First-person movement supports normal forward steering up to 90 degrees,
  full-speed direction-locked movement around the complete stick circle, a
  steerable reverse jog, and a short forward-to-back side-flip window. Mario's
  body stays locked to the selected HMD/controller direction while horizontal
  travel steers smoothly at Mario's native turning rate and landing momentum
  remains independent.
  The forward prime lasts 12 gameplay simulation ticks and the backpedal
  side-flip grace lasts 10 gameplay simulation ticks. These are Mario's
  30 Hz updates, not headset render frames, so Quest refresh rate does not
  shorten either window.
- Swimming, Wing Cap flight, and shell riding follow the direction you look.
  Normal action buttons are still used for swimming strokes and other moves.
- Tunnels, conversations, doors, and other scripted camera events continue
  running without pulling the first-person headset view away from the player.
- The character's head and normal arms are hidden in first person. Tracked,
  player-colored gloves remain visible. Torso and legs can be toggled.


CANNONS

1. Enter a cannon normally.
2. When aiming begins, the virtual view aligns horizontally with the cannon's
   forward firing angle.
3. Move your head to aim and press Jump (Right Primary by default) to fire.

Head movement remains free, but the shot stays inside the cannon's original
firing cone. Looking outside that cone gradually darkens the view. A rotating
Mario camera-arrow points back toward the nearest valid firing direction.


DEFAULT VR CONTROLS

Move                    Left stick
Smooth turn / camera    Right stick
Jump                    Right Primary (usually A)
Attack / interact       Right Secondary (usually B)
Crouch                  Left Trigger
L button                Left stick click
R button                Right stick click
Pause                   Left Menu
Close fist / grab       Hold that hand's Grip
Right Trigger punch     Disabled by default; optional in Controller Settings

Open Settings > VR > Controller Settings to remap the sticks and buttons.
A connected gamepad can remain active alongside the VR controllers.


PHYSICAL ACTIONS

PUNCH
Hold a grip to close that glove, then make a deliberate punch. The tracked
fist is the attack point. Mario's body does not have to face the target.

GRAB AND THROW
Close either grip while the glove overlaps something the game allows Mario
to pick up, such as a Bob-omb, Mips, or a baby penguin. Keep holding to carry
it at your hand. Release gently to drop it, or release during a faster hand
movement to throw it.

DIVE
Punch with both hands at nearly the same time. In the air this performs a
dive. While running fast enough, it performs a ground running dive. The two
types can be enabled separately in Motion Control Settings.

CROUCH AND GROUND POUND
With Physical Crouching / Ground Pounds enabled, lower your headset by about
one third of your calibrated standing height. Mario stays crouched until you
stand back up. Crouching while airborne triggers a ground pound.

BOWSER
Reach either glove to Bowser's tail and hold that grip. Move the held hand in
a turning arc around your body to build spin speed, then release the grip to
throw. The camera stick can also help turn. Spin acceleration and maximum
speed are adjustable.

HOOT AND MOVING ACTORS
Grip a supported interaction point to attach to it. Release the grip to let
go. Physical interactions use the game's normal interaction and networking
paths where possible.

PHYSICAL CLIMBING
Close a grip while the matching glove touches a native pole, tree, or
hangable surface. Pull or move your hands to climb. For a ledge, physically
lift or move your headset over the top of the ledge, then let go of the grip
to climb up onto it. The optional cheat permits deliberate fresh-grip wall
and ceiling contacts, but does not turn empty air or boxes into climbables.


VR SETTINGS

Camera Settings
  Camera mode, distance, per-character height, forward/back position,
  movement calibration, and field of view.

Controller Settings
  Motion-controller input, stick selection, button mapping, and optional
  Right Trigger punching.

Motion Control Settings
  Punching, grabbing, motion dives, punch sound, punch requirements,
  collider length, and Bowser spin tuning.

Model Settings
  Body Settings and Hand Settings. Body Settings includes torso/legs, the
  optional Feet Only view, crawling and mounted-action visibility, and body
  placement. Hand Settings contains glove size, rotation, and positions.

Performance
  Headset render scale. Lower it if a dense level cannot maintain a
  comfortable frame rate; menus remain at full resolution.

Cheats
  Flying Speed (%) scales VR Wing Cap flight from 100% to 300%. The multiplier
  is applied to the native flight velocity and does not accumulate each frame.
  Swimming Speed also ranges from 100% to 300%; Running Speed is capped at
  200%. Both remain at their native 100% defaults.

HUD Settings
  HUD opacity.

Experimental
  Flat first person, True First Person, True Diving (Camera Effect), Arms
  Mode, and original Mario movement.

Immersion
  Crouch / Sand Camera, Face-Stuck Blackout, Cannon Aim Direction Cone,
  Head-Tracked 3D Sound, Side-Flip Camera Follow, 180 Degree Wall-Jump Camera
  Turn, Physical Crouching / Ground Pounds, and optional Camera on Body During
  Climb Up. The climb-up camera option is disabled by default.

Effects
  Twirl Tornado Effect is enabled by default. During Shy Guy, Spindrift, and
  Tweester twirls, a small collisionless rotating white tornado remains at the
  character's feet for the full action. It is 25% transparent and scales to
  roughly 75% of the selected character.

True First Person follows animated body movement and might cause sickness.
True Diving follows the animated dive position only during dives and may also
be uncomfortable. Both options are disabled by default.
Every submenu has a Set to Defaults button.


SUCCESSFUL-EXIT CAP GESTURE

During a successful star exit, bring either tracked glove to the headset or
just above it and hold that hand's Grip + Trigger. Either hand can pull off the
cap and hold it for about one minute. Let go to drop the cap onto level
geometry; it rests briefly and fades. Death and Exit Course do not activate it.


STARTUP SHADER CACHE

The game prepares common shaders when it starts. New shader definitions found
during play are stored in gfx_shader_cache.txt and prepared on later launches.
This is intended to reduce repeat stutters in levels and mods you have used.


KNOWN LIMITATIONS

- This build targets Quest 3 standalone and remains experimental.
- Online co-op, mods, and long play sessions need more standalone testing.
- True First Person, True Diving, and Arms Mode are experimental.
- The 3D star models do not currently appear on the Star Select screen in VR.
- Some original flat billboard objects can look unusual in stereo VR.


TROUBLESHOOTING

- If the app cannot find its files after reinstalling, select the ROM again.
- For diagnostics, connect the Quest with ADB and capture Android logcat.

- Include your headset, OpenXR runtime, GPU, reproduction steps, and relevant
  [VR] or [GFX] console lines when reporting a problem.
